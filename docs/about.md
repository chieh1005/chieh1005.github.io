## Research Interests

My academic journey has taken me from the study of human movement and kinesiology to the investigation of brain mechanisms underlying age-related cognitive decline. I am particularly interested in:

### Cognitive Neuroscience
Understanding how attention, memory, and executive function change across the lifespan, and how these changes relate to neurobiological mechanisms.

### Neuroimaging Methods
Developing and applying advanced fMRI and EEG analysis pipelines to map brain structure, function, and connectivity in both healthy and clinical populations.

### Exercise Neuroscience
Investigating whether and how physical activity can serve as a protective factor against cognitive decline and neurodegenerative disease.
