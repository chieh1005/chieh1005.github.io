## About Me

I'm bridging a background in kinesiology with cognitive neuroscience and statistical neuroimaging to understand how the brain ages, and how movement can protect it. My research focuses on the intersection of clinical neuroscience and human movement, using brain imaging to probe the mechanisms of neurodegenerative disease.
